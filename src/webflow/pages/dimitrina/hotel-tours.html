€49 - ARCHAEOLOGICAL BUS TOUR TO AKROTIRI EXCAVATIONS & RED BEACH

Prophet Elias Monastery - Megalochori village - Akrotiri Excavations with Guide) - Red Bench (aprox. 30 min free time, approach on foot 10 min) Perivolos Beach (free time for swimming)
‍Starting the tour we will first vist the Prophet Ellas Monastery What makes the monastery noteworthy other than its history and importance, is the fact that it is built approximately 565 meters above sea level, making it the highest point of the whole island. Megalochori village is our next stop. An extremely charming traditional settlement, almost abandoned after the big earthquake. The tour continues to Akrotiri excavations, one of the most significant archaeological sites that includes a vast structure, once habited by 200 families and preserved buried under the volcano's lava. Then, you will have free time to the Red Beach to enjoy the colorful formations of the mountain and/or visit Akrotiri Bat for coffee or lunch with Greek traditional mezedes and ouzo (not included in the price). Perivolos, with its black sandy beach comes next. There you will have enough time to enjoy swimming, explore the area and tate fresh cooked fishes at the local taverns.

€51 - ISLAND BUS TOUR: THE MAJESTIC SPOTS OF SANTORINI

Prophet Elias Monastery - Pyrgos Village - Akrotiri Excavations - Red beach (approach on foot, approx. 10min.) - Fira Town - Oia village for the sunset
The tour starts by visiting the Prophet Elias monastery and the village of Pyrgos, one of the most picturesque villages of the island. The tour continues to Akrotiri settlement excavations. The excavations at Akrotiri have uncovered one of the most important prehistoric settlements of the Aegean, The settlement was one of the major cities and ports of the Aegean, It was a crowded city of 20 acres with a remarkable social structure public roads and multi-storey buildings. Until today the excavations have revealed only a part of the prehistoric settlement. Nowadays, the archaeological site of Akrotiri, gives visitors the opportunity to admire and walk through the sheltered settlement. Then you will have free time (approx 40min) at the Red Beach or visit Akrotiri bay for coffee or lunch (not included in the price). Fira town comes next. You will have the opportunity to take pictures of the Caldera, to shop around or enjoy the traditional Santorinian cuisine. Last stop, Oia Village for the world's most famous sunset.
